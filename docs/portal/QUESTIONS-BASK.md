# Questions for Bask (how they handle billing, cancellations and refunds)

Purpose: copy proven behavior before we build our own. Ask support or your account rep. Nothing here needs a technical person on their side.

## The plain version

Some patients pay for 3, 6 or 12 months at once, and we ship their medication a few months at a time. A patient who paid for 6 months in January has received only the first 3 months of medication by April. We want to know exactly what Bask does when that patient wants to cancel, and when things go wrong (they are not prescribed, the doctor stops the medication, a shipment cannot be filled).

## Questions

| # | Question | Notes |
|---|----------|-------|
| 1 | When a patient pays for 3, 6 or 12 months up front, can they cancel? What does Bask do with the money for shipments that have not gone out yet? | |
| 2 | Does Bask ship prepaid terms all at once or in batches? Who decides the batch size, and can we change it per product? | |
| 3 | For a monthly plan: if the patient cancels after being charged but before the pharmacy has shipped, does Bask refund? Where is the cutoff (prescription sent, order processing, shipped)? | |
| 4 | If a patient is not prescribed (the doctor says no), does Bask refund automatically? Who pays the doctor visit fee in that case? | |
| 5 | If a doctor stops a medication in the middle of a prepaid term (side effects, safety), what happens to the unshipped months? | |
| 6 | If the pharmacy cannot fill an order (out of stock, state restriction), what happens to the patient's payment and the shipment? | |
| 7 | How does Bask handle auto-renewal at the end of a prepaid term? What does the patient see, and how far ahead are they warned? | |
| 8 | Where is the "I agree to no cancellation after prepaying" consent stored? Can we export it? | |
| 9 | What does Bask's cancel button actually do for a prepaid patient: hide, turn off renewal only, or request staff review? | |
| 10 | How does Bask record what the doctor network costs us per order, and in what screens can we see profit per patient? (We are copying this ledger.) | |
| 11 | How do chargebacks and disputes get handled and recorded on prepaid terms? | |
| 12 | Can we export, for every existing patient: plan, term dates, how many batches shipped, payment history, coupons used, and consent records? (Needed for the migration.) | |
