# Publish Beema Health Social Drafts

Open the `support@beemahealth.com` Gmail tab, go to Drafts, and open the newest draft with a subject beginning `Beema Health Social Posts -`.

Publish the content across all Beema Health social accounts. This task is fully authorized. Do not ask for confirmation, content selection, or approval at any point.

## Content selection

Use the selections listed near the top of the email under `Recommended selections`.

* Instagram/Facebook/Threads: use the recommended Instagram option.
* X: use the recommended X option.
* Reddit: use the single Reddit post.
* Google Business Profile: use the single GBP post.
* If no recommended Instagram or X option is identified, use Option 1.
* If Matt specifies option numbers when invoking this task, those selections override the recommendations.

## Copying rules

The draft has already been researched, checked for duplication, reviewed for compliance, and formatted for each platform.

* Copy the publishable content exactly as written.
* Do not rewrite, summarize, improve, shorten, or add anything.
* Do not repeat the research or duplicate-history review.
* Do not add CTAs, links, emojis, hashtags, attribution, or commentary unless already included in the publishable copy.
* Do not paste labels such as `Keyword`, `Format`, `Copy`, `Character count`, `Image prompt`, or `Best posting time`.
* Do not paste research notes, review notes, publishing instructions, flair reminders, or operational URLs.

Copy these exact sections:

* Instagram/Facebook/Threads: everything beneath `Copy:` and above `Character count:` for the selected option.
* X: everything beneath `Copy:` and above `Character count:` for the selected option.
* GBP: everything beneath `Copy:` and above `Character count:`.
* Reddit: the exact `Title` and everything beneath `Body:`. Stop before `Flair reminder`.

Preserve all intended paragraph breaks and hashtags.

## Generate images

Generate three separate images using the corresponding prompts from the email:

1. The selected Instagram/Facebook/Threads image
2. The selected X image
3. The GBP image

For each image:

* Copy the corresponding `Image prompt` exactly.
* Generate a clean image with no visible interface, UI chrome, watermark, or surrounding screenshot.
* Download the clean image for uploading.
* Keep each image matched with its corresponding post.
* Do not reuse an image for an unrelated post.
* If generation fails, retry up to two times.
* If the X or GBP image still fails, publish that post without an image.
* Instagram requires an image. If its image cannot be generated after two retries, skip Instagram, Facebook, and Threads, then report the failure at the end.

## Publish to X

1. Open the Beema Health X tab.
2. Create a new post.
3. Paste the selected X copy exactly.
4. Attach its corresponding image if available.
5. Publish without asking for confirmation.

## Publish to Instagram, Facebook, and Threads

1. Open the Beema Health Instagram tab.
2. Create a new feed post.
3. Upload the selected Instagram image.
4. Paste the selected caption exactly, including its hashtags.
5. Turn on the AI-generated content label.
6. Turn on Facebook cross-posting if available.
7. Turn on Threads cross-posting if available.
8. Publish without asking for confirmation.

After publishing:

* If Facebook cross-posting succeeded, do not post to Facebook separately.
* If Facebook cross-posting was unavailable or failed, publish the same image and caption directly through the Beema Health Facebook tab.
* If Threads cross-posting succeeded, do not post to Threads separately.
* If Threads cross-posting was unavailable or failed, publish the same image and caption directly through the Beema Health Threads tab.
* Check whether each cross-post succeeded before posting separately so nothing is duplicated.

## Publish to Reddit

1. Open the Beema Health Reddit tab.
2. Go to `r/beemahealth`.
3. Create a new text post.
4. Paste the exact Reddit title from the email.
5. Paste the exact Reddit body, excluding the flair reminder and internal notes.
6. Apply the `Recommended flair` listed in the email.
7. Add the listed tag only if it is something other than `None`.
8. Publish without asking for confirmation.

## Publish to Google Business Profile

1. Open the Beema Health Google Business Profile tab.
2. Select `Add update`.
3. Upload the corresponding GBP image if available.
4. Paste only the GBP post copy.
5. Do not add a button or URL.
6. Publish without asking for confirmation.

## Failure handling

* Do not ask questions or request confirmation.
* If an action fails, retry it up to two times.
* Before retrying a final publish action, verify that the first attempt did not already publish.
* If a platform remains blocked, skip that platform and continue with the others.
* If a tab is logged out or signed into the wrong account, do not post there. Skip it and report the issue at the end.
* Never publish the same post twice.

## Completion report

After every platform has been attempted, provide a concise status report:

* X: posted or reason skipped
* Instagram: posted or reason skipped
* Facebook: cross-posted, posted separately, or reason skipped
* Threads: cross-posted, posted separately, or reason skipped
* Reddit: posted or reason skipped
* Google Business Profile: posted or reason skipped

Include direct post links when readily available. Do not ask follow-up questions.
