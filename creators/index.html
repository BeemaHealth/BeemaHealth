Share an honest, well-disclosed look at modern online care with your audience through a paid creator partnership.

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Apply

    Tell us about your audience and content. Applying is free and there is no minimum spend.

  2. Step 2

    Get reviewed

    We review your public profile and recent content, then send the creator agreement and claim guidelines if approved.

  3. Step 3

    Share with disclosure

    Promote Beema Health with your own tracking link, disclosing the paid partnership clearly in every post.

Who we are looking for

  • Current Beema Health patients. We only commission videos from people who have used our care.
  • At least 5,000 followers on TikTok or Instagram.
  • 18 or older, US-based, and located in a state where the program is available.
  • Active, original content about fitness, wellness, weight loss, or lifestyle.
  • Authentic, engaged audiences. We value engagement over raw follower count.
  • Licensed healthcare professionals and Beema Health and Care Partner staff are not eligible.

Ground rules

  • Every post clearly discloses the paid partnership, in the same place and format as the endorsement.
  • No health advice, no medical claims, and no requests for followers' health information.
  • No guaranteed results, no weight-loss numbers, and no comparisons to brand-name medications.
  • Creators never receive patient information, including whether anyone became a patient.
  • Beema Health may limit or exclude certain states.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?
Apply using the button above. We review every application and follow up by email.
How does payment work?
Compensation terms are shared in the creator agreement after approval and are paid on a regular schedule after eligibility checks. We do not publish earnings figures.
What does it cost to join?
Nothing. There is no fee and no minimum spend.
How long does approval take?
We aim to review applications within about five business days.
I am a current patient. Can I join?
Yes. The creator program is for current patients. A video about Beema Health has to come from someone who has used the care.
Earnings are not guaranteed. Compensation depends on the program terms and on actual, eligible new patients, and many creators earn nothing. Taking part is optional and never affects your care, prices, or eligibility. Providers and pharmacies are not compensated for any program, and clinical decisions are made independently.
