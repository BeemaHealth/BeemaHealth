Tesamorelin treats visceral fat in HIV lipodystrophy - not general weight loss

Tesamorelin (Egrifta) is FDA-approved to reduce excess abdominal fat in HIV-associated lipodystrophy. It is not a GLP-1 or a weight-loss drug.

What tesamorelin is actually approved to do

Tesamorelin is sold in the United States as Egrifta (including Egrifta WR and Egrifta SV formulations). It is a synthetic analog of growth hormone-releasing factor. The FDA-approved use is reduction of excess abdominal fat in HIV-infected adults with lipodystrophy. That is a complication of HIV and some antiretroviral regimens, not a synonym for 'wanting a smaller waist.'

This article is educational, not medical advice. Beema Health does not offer tesamorelin. Wellness marketing that calls tesamorelin a weight-loss peptide skips the limitation of use printed in the label: it is not indicated for weight-loss management and has a weight-neutral effect on the scale.

Why tesamorelin is not in the GLP-1 family

GLP-1 receptor agonists act on incretin pathways that reduce appetite and slow gastric emptying. Tesamorelin acts on the pituitary to increase endogenous growth hormone, which then raises IGF-1. Those are different hormones, different receptors, and different trial endpoints. Grouping tesamorelin with semaglutide because both are 'injections about fat' is biologically false.

People searching GLP-1 peptides for weight loss should not be steered toward tesamorelin as a substitute. If the clinical goal is chronic weight management, the evidence base that regulators and obesity guidelines discuss is incretin-based therapy, lifestyle support, and sometimes other labeled obesity medicines - not HIV lipodystrophy therapy.

  • Tesamorelin: GHRH analog for HIV-associated visceral fat, daily injection, weight-neutral on label
  • Semaglutide/tirzepatide class: incretin agonists studied for percent body-weight change
  • Beema Health offering: neither tesamorelin nor branded Egrifta

Limitations of use you will not see in a peptide ad

The Egrifta WR prescribing information is explicit. Long-term cardiovascular safety has not been established. Clinicians are told to weigh continuation if visceral fat does not clearly improve. There are no data that tesamorelin improves adherence to antiretroviral therapy. Formulations differ in milligram strength, reconstitution, and storage; Egrifta WR and Egrifta SV are not substitutable.

Glucose monitoring matters because growth-hormone axis stimulation can worsen hyperglycemia. People without HIV lipodystrophy who buy 'tesamorelin for fat loss' from a research shop take on those endocrine risks without the labeled benefit that justified approval.

Egrifta is a prescription product dispensed by licensed pharmacies for an approved (or carefully justified off-label) use. Vials marketed as research tesamorelin are not that product. Frequently asked questions

Is tesamorelin a GLP-1 medication for weight loss?
No. Tesamorelin is a growth hormone-releasing factor analog. It is not a GLP-1, GIP, or glucagon receptor agonist. FDA labeling for Egrifta WR states it is indicated to reduce excess abdominal fat in HIV-infected adults with lipodystrophy and is not indicated for weight-loss management. The label describes a weight-neutral effect.

Why do some wellness clinics advertise tesamorelin for belly fat?
The approved indication is a specific HIV-associated fat-distribution condition, not cosmetic abdominal fat in people without that diagnosis. Using tesamorelin for general fat loss is off-label and is not how Beema Health practices. Off-label use still requires a licensed clinician, a valid prescription, and an honest risk discussion. It is not a DIY peptide protocol.
Does tesamorelin make you lose scale weight?
The FDA label says tesamorelin is not indicated for weight-loss management and notes a weight-neutral effect. Studies supporting approval focused on visceral adipose tissue in HIV lipodystrophy, not on percent total body-weight loss of the kind reported in STEP or SURMOUNT trials of incretin medicines.
What are important tesamorelin safety points from the label?
The Egrifta WR label warns that long-term cardiovascular safety has not been established. Glucose can rise because the drug increases IGF-1 through growth-hormone pathways. It is a daily abdominal injection with specific reconstitution rules. Egrifta WR and Egrifta SV are not interchangeable. This page is not a complete label; patients who are prescribed tesamorelin need the official Medication Guide.
If I want medical weight loss, what should I read instead?

Those medicines work through incretin pathways and are evaluated for chronic weight management in a completely different evidence base.
