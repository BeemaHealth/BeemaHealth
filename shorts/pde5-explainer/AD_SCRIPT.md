# Beema Health: sildenafil + tadalafil ad (vertical, ~50 s)

Status: DRAFT for compliance review. Not legal advice. Run through the legitscript-compliance, meta-ads-compliance and google-ads-compliance skills and counsel before spend.

## Creative approach (from market research, 2026)
- Convenience + transparent price is the lane that fits a cash-pay telehealth brand. Humor/innuendo (BlueChew, Rugiet, Numan) and celebrity (Ro) are what the big players use, but innuendo is a platform-policy and "personal attributes" risk and is not used here.
- Hook = a concrete, visual pattern interrupt in the first 2 seconds ("Skip the waiting room"), not a question about the viewer's body. No second-person condition claims ("Do you have ED?").
- Native-feeling vertical 3D motion graphics, kinetic text, price shown on screen, then a full safety card. Creative testing should vary the hook and the first 3 seconds (per the Bask telehealth blog guidance).

## Compliance guardrails applied
- Generic names only. No brand names, no brand colours/shapes (tablets are plain white round, not blue diamond / yellow almond trade dress).
- Both products are FDA-approved GENERICS (docs/features/treatment-pages.md, corrected 2026-09-03), not compounded: the compounded-GLP-1 sentences do not apply, and no "same as" claim is made.
- No outcome, speed, duration, performance or confidence claims. "Never guaranteed" and "a provider decides" are stated.
- Indication stated (erectile dysfunction); important safety information shown AND voiced (nitrates/riociguat, priapism >4 h, sudden vision/hearing loss, common side effects, tell provider). FDA is actively pursuing telehealth drug advertising and the "adequate provision" standard is under rulemaking, so full on-screen safety is used instead of relying on a shortened statement.
- No LegitScript seal or certification claim in the ad (seal is for the certified site only).
- No before/after, no testimonials, no models, no anatomy.
- Prices copied from src/lib/simple-treatment-pricing.ts (Matt's ED sheet, 2026-10-27). Re-verify at publish.
- Adults 18+ only audience; Rx ads need Meta authorization (LegitScript active) and Google pre-approval; TikTok policy for Rx is unclear in sources: confirm with TikTok before running there.
