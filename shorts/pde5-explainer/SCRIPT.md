# How sildenafil and tadalafil work (PDE5 inhibition)

Educational explainer, not a sales piece. No brand, price, or call to action in the video.

## Facts and sources
- Sexual stimulation releases nitric oxide (NO) from nerve endings and endothelium in the corpus cavernosum. NO activates soluble guanylate cyclase in smooth muscle, which makes cGMP from GTP. cGMP activates protein kinase G, which lowers intracellular calcium and relaxes the smooth muscle. Arteries dilate, the sinusoids fill, and the swelling compresses the draining veins. (Lue TF, N Engl J Med 2000;342:1802-13; Andersson KE, Pharmacol Rev 2011;63:811-59.)
- PDE5 hydrolyses cGMP to inactive 5'-GMP. Sildenafil and tadalafil are competitive PDE5 inhibitors, so cGMP breaks down more slowly. They need NO / stimulation to have an effect. (Corbin JD, Francis SH, Int J Clin Pract 2002;56:453-9; Boolell M et al., Int J Impot Res 1996;8:47-52.)
- Half-life: sildenafil about 4 h, tadalafil about 17.5 h; tadalafil label describes activity up to 36 h. (FDA labels for Viagra and Cialis.)
- Nitrates plus PDE5 inhibitors: contraindicated, risk of severe hypotension. (FDA labels.)
- Sildenafil was first studied for angina, then repurposed. Same PDE5 enzyme is in lung vessels (PAH use). Not used in script to stay tight.

## Narration (voice.json drives timing; scene length = audio + pad)
1. hook: Sildenafil and tadalafil don't create an erection. They stop your body from switching one off.
2. vessel: An erection is a blood flow event. Inside the penis, two spongy chambers are fed by arteries wrapped in smooth muscle. At rest, that muscle stays tense, and the arteries stay narrow.
3. signal: With arousal, nerves and the vessel lining release nitric oxide. It slips into the muscle cells and switches on an enzyme that makes a messenger molecule called cyclic G M P.
4. relax: Cyclic G M P tells the muscle to relax. Calcium inside the cell drops, the arteries widen, blood fills the chambers, and the swelling squeezes the exit veins shut.
5. pde5: But the body always builds in an off switch. An enzyme called P D E 5 chops cyclic G M P into an inactive fragment, and the signal fades.
6. inhibit: Sildenafil and tadalafil are P D E 5 inhibitors. They compete for the enzyme's active site, so cyclic G M P is broken down more slowly, and the relaxation signal lasts longer.
7. timing: Nothing starts without stimulation, because no nitric oxide means no signal to protect. The big difference is time. Sildenafil's half-life is about four hours. Tadalafil's is about seventeen.
8. safety: These drugs also widen other blood vessels, which is why mixing them with nitrate medicines can drop blood pressure dangerously. Same enzyme, same vessels, different timing.
