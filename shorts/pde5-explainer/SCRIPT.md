# How sildenafil and tadalafil work (PDE5 inhibition)

Educational explainer, not a sales piece. No brand, price, or call to action in the video.

## Facts and sources
- Sexual stimulation releases nitric oxide (NO) from nerve endings and endothelium in the corpus cavernosum. NO activates soluble guanylate cyclase in smooth muscle, which makes cGMP from GTP. cGMP activates protein kinase G, which lowers intracellular calcium and relaxes the smooth muscle. Arteries dilate, the sinusoids fill, and the swelling compresses the draining veins. (Lue TF, N Engl J Med 2000;342:1802-13; Andersson KE, Pharmacol Rev 2011;63:811-59.)
- PDE5 hydrolyses cGMP to inactive 5'-GMP. Sildenafil and tadalafil are competitive PDE5 inhibitors, so cGMP breaks down more slowly. They need NO / stimulation to have an effect. (Corbin JD, Francis SH, Int J Clin Pract 2002;56:453-9; Boolell M et al., Int J Impot Res 1996;8:47-52.)
- Half-life: sildenafil about 4 h, tadalafil about 17.5 h; tadalafil label describes activity up to 36 h. (FDA labels for Viagra and Cialis.)
- Nitrates plus PDE5 inhibitors: contraindicated, risk of severe hypotension. (FDA labels.)
- Sildenafil was first studied for angina, then repurposed. Same PDE5 enzyme is in lung vessels (PAH use). Not used in script to stay tight.

## Narration (voice.json drives timing; scene length = audio + short pad)
Wording is deliberately clinical and family friendly: "erectile tissue" and "blood flow", no anatomy shown.
1. hook: Sildenafil and tadalafil don't create blood flow on their own. They stop your body from switching one signal off.
2. vessel: Healthy erectile function depends on blood flow. Zoom into one of the small arteries that supply the tissue. It's wrapped in smooth muscle, which at rest stays tense, so the artery stays narrow.
3. signal: When the body sends the right signal, nerves and the vessel lining release nitric oxide. It enters the muscle cell and switches on an enzyme that makes a messenger molecule, cyclic G M P.
4. relax: Cyclic G M P tells the muscle to relax. Calcium leaves the cell, the artery widens, and more blood flows in.
5. pde5: But the body builds in an off switch. An enzyme called P D E 5 breaks cyclic G M P down, and the signal fades.
6. inhibit: Sildenafil and tadalafil are P D E 5 inhibitors. These blue molecules fit into the enzyme's active site and block it, so cyclic G M P lasts longer, and the artery stays relaxed.
7. timing: They only work with the body's own signal, because without nitric oxide there's nothing to protect. The main difference is time. Sildenafil's half-life is about four hours. Tadalafil's is about seventeen.
8. safety: These drugs also relax other vessels, which is why mixing them with nitrate medicines can drop blood pressure dangerously. Same enzyme, same vessels, different timing.
